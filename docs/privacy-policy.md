# Vesper Cine – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 7 October 2026 · Developer: [YOUR NAME / STUDIO] · Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL]*

Vesper Cine ("the app") is a camera app. This policy explains what data the app uses and what leaves your phone.

## What stays on your phone

- **Camera.** The app uses the camera to show the viewfinder and record video.
- **Microphone.** The app uses the microphone to record the audio track of your clips.
- **Your clips.** Videos are saved on your phone in *Movies/Vesper Cine*. The app never uploads your videos, photos or audio anywhere.
- **Calibration data.** The hot-pixel and noise calibration of your phone's sensor is stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
- **Settings and plan status.** Your settings, the number of free clips used and whether you unlocked Pro are stored on your phone.

The app does not ask for an account, your name, your contacts, or your location.

## What may leave your phone

1. **Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, Google).** If the app crashes, technical details are sent to Google Firebase: the crash location (stack trace), phone model, Android version, app version, and a random installation identifier. This helps fix bugs. It contains no footage, location or personal content. You can switch this off at any time in *Settings › Info › Send crash reports*. See [Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).
2. **Purchases (Google Play Billing).** If you buy Vesper Pro, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether the purchase exists. We do not receive your payment details.
3. **Ads (Google AdMob).** The free plan shows a short rewarded ad when your free clips are used up (not while recording). Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice later in *Settings › Vesper Pro › Ad choices*. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. Pro removes all ads. See [Google's ads policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data for anything other than the purposes above.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country).

## Your choices and rights

- Turn crash reports off in the app; decline ad personalisation; reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
- Crash data held by Google can be deleted on request: contact us at the address above and give the app version and approximate date.
- Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Your clips remain in *Movies/Vesper Cine* until you delete them.
- If you are in the EEA/UK you have rights of access, correction and deletion under data-protection law; contact us to use them.
